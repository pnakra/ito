## 2. Short answer at the top (first thing on the page)

> **Short answer:** When it comes to anything physical, maybe isn't yes. It means they haven't decided, so the move is to slow down and let them decide without pressure. If you only get a yes after pushing, that isn't a real yes.

## 3. Common questions (new section near the end, before the ito link)

**Does maybe mean yes?**
Not for anything physical. A yes sounds like a yes. Maybe means they're unsure, and unsure means wait.

**Does maybe mean no?**
Sometimes. Some people say maybe because a direct no feels awkward or unsafe. Treat it as "not now" and let them bring it back up if they want to.

**What should I say when someone says maybe?**
Something low pressure, like "No rush, we don't have to do anything." Then actually mean it.

**Is it ok to ask again later?**
Once, at a different time, without pressure, is fine. Asking over and over until the answer changes is pushing.

**What if they said maybe and then went along with it?**
Going along with something isn't the same as wanting it. If you're not sure how it went for them, check in, and think it through with ito.
